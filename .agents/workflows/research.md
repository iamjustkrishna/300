# Workflow: Research & Market Analysis

## Objective
Analyze competitor landscape, user review complaints on the Google Play Store, and pricing benchmarks to position 300 for commercial and user retention success.

## Leading Agent
`research-agent.md`

## Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Competitor Teardown
1. Examine Top Habit/Discipline Apps:
   - Strengths: Streak visualizations, simple daily prompts.
   - Weaknesses: Cartoonish avatars, bright childish palettes, annoying notifications, expensive recurring subscriptions.
2. Examine Top Wallpaper & Personalization Apps:
   - Strengths: Large volume of assets.
   - Weaknesses: Riddled with interstitial video ads, slow loading, low-res images, broken widgets, zero philosophical or emotional cohesion.

### Step 2: Extract Market Insights
- Users who seek discipline want an austere, respectful, dark aesthetic.
- A widget on the home screen serves as a persistent subconscious anchor.
- Fast, ad-free, 100% reliable 1-tap wallpaper setting creates immense delight.

### Step 3: Synthesis & Documentation
- Document competitor insights in `.agents/specs/product-spec.md` under Competitive Analysis.
- Share monetization findings with `product-agent.md`.
