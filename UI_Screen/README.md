# 300 — Classical Stoic Luxury UI Screens (Phase 1 Approved Redesign)

This folder contains the completely redesigned, museum-grade, classical stoic luxury UI screens for the **300** Android app, generated using Stitch's flagship model (**GEMINI_3_8_FLASH**) and inspired by the user's `*_v2.png` references and top minimalist design leaders (*Nothing OS, Linear, Bang & Olufsen*).

---

### Design System: Classical Stoic Luxury
* **Canvas**: Deep Basalt & Pitch-Black OLED (`#08080A` / `#0B0B0C`) with subtle warm specular ambient rim glow.
* **Surfaces**: Sculpted Dark Basalt Stone (`#121216`) with 16dp–18dp rounded corners and delicate hairline borders (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.12)`).
* **Primary Accent**: Antique Brushed Satin Gold & Spartan Bronze (`#C5A869`) with subtle metallic directional gradients.
* **Secondary Jewel Accent**: Deep Spartan Oxblood / Crimson (`#8A1C14`).
* **Typography**:
  * **Headlines / Brand**: Exquisite Classical Luxury Serif (*EB Garamond* / *Bodoni Moda* style) with generous tracking.
  * **Body / UI Labels**: Ultra-clean modern sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter*).
* **Philosophy**: Stoic grandeur, quiet architectural discipline, tactile stonework, zero gaming clutter, seamless 1-tap Android transformation.

## Detailed Architectural Screen Breakdown

### 01. Splash Screen (`01_Splash_Brand_Genesis.png`)
* **Visual Anchor**: Circular antique bronze hoplite shield medallion with vertical spear piercing and embossed Spartan $\Lambda$ (Lambda) chevron with chiseled bevels and antique gold patina.
* **Typography**: Centered commanding `300` in classical luxury serif (*EB Garamond*), spaced `SPARTA THEME` with delicate gold hairline divider, and stoic micro-motto *"RETURN WITH YOUR SHIELD, OR ON IT."*
* **Loading Component**: Dual-tone progress bar (brushed satin gold to dark bronze) with tracking-widest micro-caption `PREPARING YOUR SET · V1.0 × PHALANX`.

### 02. Onboarding Screen (`02_Onboarding_The_Reveal.png`)
* **Hero Mockup**: Flagship smartphone floating inside an ancient stone portico sanctuary with monumental archways, quiet reflective water pool, and glowing fire basin. Display inside phone features a stoic minimal lock screen (*"A CALMER BRIGHTER YOU"*).
* **Narrative**: Classical serif headline *"Make your phone feel intentional."* and subtitle *"Themes, widgets, and icons — curated as one set."*
* **Feature Pillars**: 3 gold circular wireframe chips:
  1. `True black` (crescent moon)
  2. `Native widgets` (4-square grid)
  3. `One-tap sets` (layered stack)
* **Primary Action**: Full-width brushed satin-gold pill button: `Start exploring →`.

### 03. Home Dashboard (`03_Home_Curated_Themes.png`)
* **Top App Bar**: Spartan Lambda crest, `300 CURATED THEMES`, search glass pill, and profile indicator.
* **The Daily Set Hero**: Cinematic feature card *"Obsidian Ritual"* depicting an ancient monumental colonnade archway with fire braziers and antique bronze shield. Includes `Preview theme >` button and `99.2% Black · 4K UHD` badge.
* **Filter Rail**: Horizontal pill switcher: `Featured` (active solid gold), `AMOLED`, `Armor`, `Minimal`.
* **Curated Archive**: 2-column masonry grid cards:
  1. *Spartan Dawn* ("Light finds a way.")
  2. *Helm* ("Silence looks stronger.")
  3. *Aegean Noir* ("Depth over noise.")
  4. *Last Light* ("Stillness remains.")
* **Bottom Dock**: 4-tab docked navigation: `Discover` (active), `Studio`, `Vault`, `Settings`.

### 04. Theme Live Preview & 1-Tap Apply (`04_Theme_Live_Preview_Apply.png`)
* **Top Bar**: Back chevron, classical serif title `Obsidian Ritual (CURATED THEME · 088)`, and favorite heart.
* **Segmented Mode Switcher**: `Home screen` (active brushed gold) vs `Lock screen` (dark basalt).
* **Interactive Live Phone Mockup**: Shows the exact live theme with arched portal wallpaper, stoic digital clock widget (`09:41 · Tue, Apr 22`), and dock of 5 bronze glyph icons.
* **Badges**: `TRUE BLACK`, `NATIVE WIDGET`, `BRASS GLYPHS`.
* **Component List**: Live inspectable rows for Wallpaper, Clock widget, and Icon pack with chevrons.
* **Master CTA**: Large brushed satin-gold pill button: `Apply this set →` with micro-caption `1-TAP ANDROID SYSTEM APPLIER · ZERO ROOT REQUIRED`.

### 05. Studio — Widgets & Wallpapers (`05_Studio_Widgets_Wallpapers.png`)
* **Header & Status**: Classical serif `Studio / SHAPES A CALMER TOMORROW` with gold laurel badge `300 SET ACTIVE`.
* **Category Switcher**: Dual-pill toggle: `Widgets` (active) vs `Wallpapers`.
* **Featured Widget Forge Cards**:
  1. *Monolith Clock (2x2)*: Obsidian marble widget with Roman numerals (`XII`, `III`, `VI`, `IX`), analog hands, digital readout, stoic quote *"Time keeps better company here."*, and `+ Add to home` gold button.
  2. *Shield Battery (2x2)*: Sculpted bronze hoplite shield with Lambda, 78% battery readout, crimson power arc, quote *"Power fuels a quieter path."*, and `+ Add to home` gold button.
* **Wallpaper Sets**: 2-column grid featuring *Obsidian Sanctuary* and *Crimson Silence* (Vanguard Pass locked).
* **Bottom Dock**: Active `Studio` Doric temple tab glowing in gold.

### 06. Settings & Vanguard Lifetime Pass (`06_Settings_Vanguard_Pass.png`)
* **Top Bar**: `Settings` in classical serif with `v1.0.0` metallic badge.
* **Vanguard Pass Hero**: High-contrast basalt card featuring the ancient weathered bronze Corinthian helmet with crimson crest, laurel wreath badge `LIFETIME ACCESS`, title `Vanguard Pass`, subtitle *"A complete collection. Yours for life."*, price `₹199 one-time` (zero recurring subscriptions), and `Unlock the full vault >` CTA button.
* **Display Section**: Pure black canvas (gold switch ON) and Low-power mode (switch OFF).
* **Library Section**: Offline cache, Restore purchases, and About 300 rows.
* **Bottom Dock**: Active `Settings` gear tab glowing in gold.
