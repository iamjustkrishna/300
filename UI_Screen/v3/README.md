# 300 — Classical Stoic Luxury UI Screens (v3 Suite)

This directory contains the **v3 UI Suite** for the **300** Android application, uniting the quiet luxury and minimal elegance of the `UI_Screen/` layout standards with the battle-tested, chiseled Spartan iconography from `inspiration/` (the iconic Corinthian bronze helmet, forged hoplite shield with Greek meander bevels, thrusting spear perspective, and drifting embers).

All screens were generated using Stitch's flagship model (**GEMINI_3_8_FLASH**) inside Project `14386677080154071069`.

---

## Design System: Classical Stoic Luxury (v3)

* **Canvas**: Deep 100% OLED Pitch-Black Basalt (`#08080A` / `#0B0B0C`) with subtle warm specular ambient rim lighting and drifting ember sparks.
* **Surfaces**: Sculpted Dark Basalt Stone (`#121216`) with 16dp–18dp rounded corners and delicate hairline borders (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.12)`).
* **Primary Accent**: Antique Brushed Satin Gold & Spartan Bronze (`#C5A869`) with specular directional gleam (`#E2CA8F`).
* **Secondary Jewel Accent**: Deep Spartan Oxblood Crimson (`#8A1C14`).
* **Typography**:
  * **Headlines / Display**: Classical Luxury Roman Serif (*EB Garamond* / *Cinzel* style) with generous tracking.
  * **Body / UI Labels**: Ultra-clean Modern Sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter*).
* **Philosophy**: Stoic discipline, architectural majesty, tactile bronze and stone textures, zero clutter, seamless 1-tap Android transformation.

## Detailed Architectural Screen Breakdown

### 01. Splash Screen (`01_Splash_Brand_Genesis_v3.png`)
* **Visual Anchor**: Circular antique bronze hoplite shield medallion with outer rivets and concentric meander rings, vertical spear pointing upwards, and embossed satin gold $\Lambda$ (Lambda) chevron.
* **Typography**: Centered commanding `300` in classical luxury serif, spaced `SPARTA THEME` with delicate gold diamond divider, and stoic micro-motto *"THIS IS SPARTA. RETURN WITH YOUR SHIELD, OR ON IT."*
* **Loading Component**: Dual-tone progress bar with micro-caption `PREPARING YOUR SET · V3.0 × PHALANX` and floating golden ember sparks.

### 02. Onboarding Screen (`02_Onboarding_The_Reveal_v3.png`)
* **Hero Mockup**: Flagship smartphone floating gracefully over a tranquil reflective water pool inside monumental stone arches, with glowing flame braziers and warm sunset atmosphere.
* **Inside Phone Display**: Minimal lock screen (*"Mon, Apr 28 · A CALMER BRIGHTER YOU"*), weather forecast, search pill, and 4 bronze glyph dock icons.
* **Narrative**: Classical serif headline *"Make your phone feel intentional."* and subtitle *"Themes, widgets, and icons — curated as one set."*
* **3 Feature Pillars**: Circular gold wireframe cards for `True black`, `Native widgets`, and `One-tap sets`.
* **Primary Action**: Brushed satin-gold pill button: `START EXPLORING →`.

### 03. Home Dashboard (`03_Home_Curated_Themes_v3.png`)
* **Top App Bar**: Spartan Lambda crest, `300 CURATED THEMES`, search glass pill, and account avatar.
* **The Daily Set Hero**: Cinematic feature card *"Obsidian Ritual (THE DAILY SET · ED.088)"* depicting an ancient monumental colonnade archway with fire braziers, antique bronze shield, and `PREVIEW THEME →` button with `99.2% Black · 4K UHD` badge.
* **Filter Rail**: Horizontal pill switcher: `Featured` (active solid gold), `AMOLED`, `Armor & Helm`, `Minimal Stoic`.
* **Curated Archive**: 2-column grid cards:
  1. *Spartan Dawn* ("Light finds a way.")
  2. *Corinthian Helm* ("Silence looks stronger.")
  3. *The Phalanx* ("Depth over noise.")
  4. *Last Light* ("Stillness remains.")
* **Bottom Dock**: 4-tab docked navigation: `Discover` (active), `Studio`, `Vault`, `Settings`.

### 04. Theme Live Preview & 1-Tap Apply (`04_Theme_Live_Preview_Apply_v3.png`)
* **Top Bar**: Back chevron, classical serif title `Obsidian Ritual (CURATED THEME · 088)`, and favorite heart.
* **Segmented Mode Switcher**: `Home screen` (active brushed gold) vs `Lock screen` (dark basalt).
* **Interactive Live Phone Mockup**: Displays the battle-worn bronze Corinthian helmet wallpaper with specular rim lighting, digital clock widget (`09:41 · Sparta · 24°C`), and dock of 5 bronze glyph icons.
* **Badges**: `TRUE BLACK · 99.4%`, `NATIVE WIDGET`, `BRASS GLYPHS`.
* **Component List**: Live inspectable rows for Wallpaper, Clock widget, and Icon pack with chevrons.
* **Master CTA**: Large brushed satin-gold pill button: `Apply this set →` with micro-caption `1-TAP ANDROID SYSTEM APPLIER · ZERO ROOT REQUIRED`.

### 05. Studio — Widgets & Wallpapers (`05_Studio_Widgets_Wallpapers_v3.png`)
* **Header & Status**: Classical serif `Studio / SHAPES A CALMER TOMORROW` with gold laurel badge `300 SET ACTIVE`.
* **Category Switcher**: Dual-pill toggle: `Widgets` (active) vs `Wallpapers`.
* **Featured Widget Forge Cards**:
  1. *Monolith Clock (2x2)*: Obsidian marble widget with Roman numerals (`XII`, `III`, `VI`, `IX`), analog hands, digital readout, stoic quote *"Time keeps better company here."*, and `+ Add to home` gold button.
  2. *Shield Battery (2x2)*: Sculpted bronze hoplite shield with Lambda, 78% battery readout, crimson power arc, quote *"Power fuels a quieter path."*, and `+ Add to home` gold button.
* **Wallpaper Sets**: 2-column grid featuring *Sanctuary of Sparta* and *Crimson Silence* (Vanguard lock badge).
* **Bottom Dock**: Active `Studio` Doric temple tab glowing in gold.

### 06. Settings & Vanguard Lifetime Pass (`06_Settings_Vanguard_Pass_v3.png`)
* **Top Bar**: `Settings` in classical serif with `V1.0.0` metallic badge.
* **Vanguard Pass Hero**: High-contrast basalt card featuring the ancient weathered bronze Corinthian helmet with crimson crest, laurel wreath badge `LIFETIME ACCESS`, title `Vanguard Pass`, subtitle *"A complete collection. Yours for life."*, price `₹199 one-time` (zero recurring subscriptions), and `Unlock the full vault →` CTA button.
* **Display Section**: Pure black canvas (gold switch ON) and Low-power mode (switch OFF).

* **Bottom Dock**: Active `Settings` gear tab glowing in gold.
